# My Dad

Dad died when I was 8 so I don't have a lot of memories or photos.
I believe this is the first photo I took and it was with a box camera.
![First photo I took of dad with a box camera|365x633](My%201st%20photo.jpg)

This is where he worked in Montreal, at Northern Electric. He's on the far right.
![Where dad worked. He's on the far right|553x370](Dad%20at%20work%201956%201.jpeg)

He and mom were very involved in the Anglican Church in music. Dad could play a pipe organ and I remember sitting with him at the console while he practiced.
[Organ console - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organ_console)
