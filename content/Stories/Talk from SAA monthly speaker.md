Thanks for the opportunity to share. I went back to my 1st step share and realized I'd forgotten some of this stuff. Perhaps that is a gift of recovery - those memories have less power.
How did I get here? I turned out the way I turned out for a reason or two.

It's a few years ago and I've just turned 8 and I'm home from school for lunch. My dad is home today because he's feeling sick. My brother runs into the kitchen and yells "mom! Dad is turning purple". I run upstairs to the bedroom to see my dad dying of a massive heart attack. And it's just 6 weeks since my closest grandmother died from cancer. A few weeks later Pres. Kennedy is assassinated. Within 8 months both of my grandfathers die. I don't think I knew the phrase "shit-storm" at the time. I certainly learned about grieving.

At this time the doctors think the best thing for my mom is a lot of tranquillizers. Suddenly she is not available either. For her this eventually turns into alcoholism. My brother and I fend for ourselves.

Fast forward to the next summer and my mom takes my brother
and I to my aunt's cottage. In the main room of her cottage, the
walls are lined with playboy centerfolds. This is my first
exposure to porn. These days I wonder what happened to my aunt in WW2 that had her turn out that way. She was an alcoholic.

Now I'm 13 or 14 and my mom's male friend, takes me back to his place and sexually abuses me. Did my mom know what was going on? I never told her. I believe she'd also been sexually abused and my abuser told me how he'd been abused.

Sidebar - one of the things I've taken on in this life is the chain of abuse stops with me.

Around this time I discover my brother's porn collection. I start acting out when no one is home.

Fast forward again to the early 80's and I'm in my mid-twenties. I'm engaged to a beautiful and talented woman. She knows about my pile of porn magazines. She takes me to a documentary about porn called "not a love story". I realize now she is trying to help. I don't change anything. She calls off the ngagement and leaves.

Now it's the mid ninety's and I'm married with two kids in elementary school. We move and I pick a house that is a bit more than we can really afford because it has better internet access. I stay up late at night on the computer looking at porn.

It's a few years later, we've divorced and I'm living in a small apartment. Internet access has improved. I have the tools and time available to spend on porn. One day I stumble across an article about the effects of using porn and get some counselling. After this counselling I am sober for a few years. This councillor did not know about SAA. During this sober time I meet and marry my current wife.

At some point that I don't recall, the magic combo of HALT overcomes me and I start using again. I'm very clever and no one knows. The time I spend on porn increases and takes me away from my other interests.

It's the summer of 2016 and I'm at the CSAT reviewing the  results of my sex addict test. which I did well on btw.

Fast forward and it's the evening of Oct 5 2016 and I'm parked   across the street from St Mathews still wondering if I have a problem.

I muster the courage to walk in and am 12 stepped by Dave MJ  and another member. That was a transformational time.

I continued working with the CSAT and started on the steps.   I did my step 5 with our female minister. That was a moment!  When I got to step 8 it occurred to me as 2 sub steps.

① made a list of those we had harmed

② and became willing to make amends

I realized I didn't have much control over how the amends would happen. Probably the most grateful event was about 5 years ago when I had a chance to visit my in-laws by myself. I took that opportunity to make amends. I didn't know at the time that my father-in-law was already suffering from dementia.

Making amends to my adult children - my daughter especially -  was also challenging and they support me. My daughter has   peers dealing with addiction.

With my wife its an ongoing living amends. It takes some time to  rebuild trust.

I think someone in this group mentioned the divorce rate in the recovery groups is lower than the average for the general population.

All this has made a difference in my life  I'm sober 7 years 6m 6 days. How did that happen?

* Got a sponsor
* worked with CSAT
* Did the steps
* Go to meetings
* took on service roles
* Sponsor

I get help. I used to think that was a sign of weakness. I never  complained and tried to mask what was going on. I only found out what a migraine is and that I've been getting them since I was a teenager since I started in the program. It never occurred to me before to talk to my doctor about it. I now have great meds for it.

In addition:

* I was diagnosed with ADHD 3 years ago and have taken the meds since. I've probably mentioned there's a strong link between ADHD and addiction.
* I got my hip replaced. I can say that pain is a pain and really sucked the life out of me. What a miracle.
* I got my right hand fixed. I have a hereditary condition call Dupuytren's Contracture. My right hand had turned into a cup holder. 37 stitches but who's counting

We recently experience 3 big life events and I'm still sober the first would never have happened if I hadn't got into the program. We built a new house last year and it's multi generational. My younger son and daughter-in-law live with us  and they're expecting a daughter at the end of June.   we moved from the house we'd lived in for 18 years in  November. No stress!

My father-in-law's dementia progressed rapidly. He died  suddenly in the middle of January. Like WTFIGO We are all grieving. We just had his memorial service on Easter weekend and there were over 400 people there. No stress!

Feb 1 I had the surgery on my right hand. Good thing I'm somewhat ambidextrous. No stress!

Oh yeah forgot about Christmas and my daughter moving to the UK in January.  Anyone see a reason to act out in that shit-storm?

I'm still an addict and saying the Serenity prayer many times a day and using the 3 second rule and the other tools. For me it's one day at a time and let go and let God and easy does it

Thanks for letting me share.
