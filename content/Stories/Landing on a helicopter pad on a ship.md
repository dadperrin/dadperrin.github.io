# Landing on a helicopter pad on a ship

Landing during rough seas involved the pilot getting in sync with how the ship was rolling in the waves and then planting it.

There was a manned fire monitor in a fire suit on duty in case the helicopter caught fire on landing. In theory this was to put the fire out to save those on the helicopter. It's said the force of the water would be used to push the burning helicopter off the ship into the ocean to save the ship.
