# Float plane and grappling hook Mackenzie Delta

[Mackenzie River Delta](https://www.google.ca/maps/place/Mackenzie+River/@68.8659824,-134.9459583,8.5z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x516acfafbf62aacb:0x4f04f39af7f89de4!8m2!3d65.1361141!4d-126.3057302!16zL20vMDFsMjZs?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDQwMi4xIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)

I was asked to recover a tide gauge set up in one of the channels of the Mackenzie Delta. This was part of research relating to the drilling of the enormous gas reserves under the delta.

Once we landed on the channel where the gauge was supposed to be, I stood on the float as the float plane as it traveled the channels. I held on to the wing strut with one hand and the grappling hook with the other. There was a bit of prop wash experienced.

On one flight back to Tuk harbour, we just missed flying into a radio tower in the fog. And the wings were icing.

We didn't find the tide gauge. There was no GPS at that time to help locate stuff.
