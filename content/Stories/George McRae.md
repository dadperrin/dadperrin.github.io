# George McRae

Uncle Gorge, aka George McRae, was my God Father. He lived two doors away from us in Montreal and was a great friend of my dad.

Uncle George let me drive his car when I was about 5. I guess since he'd survived being a tank commander in WW2, this didn't seem too risky.

He had the most incredible laugh. His nickname for me was Magillacutty, which, until this moment, I have never tried to spell.
