# 4th coop workterm, Syncrude - Northward Developments Ltd.

First time to Alberta

Guy Woodward was on the plane #angel

I lived in a house trailer with another Waterloo student. Accommodation was provided by Syncrude and it was across the street from a gravel and concrete plant that ran 24x7.
![](../Files/Tray%202.1%202.jpg)

![](../Files/Tray%202.3%2051.jpg)

Ft McMurray was still a rough spot. We lived around the corner from the Riviera Hotel. If you zoom in on the bar at the right, you can see the metal doors. We didn't go there. We went to the Peter Pond bar.
![](../Files/Tray%202.1%2077.jpg)

The highway to the mine site was not yet paved. This was known as Supertest Hill.
![](../Files/Tray%202.5%2045.jpg)

It was not unusual to be stopped on the road to Edmonton by the Highway Patrol for a complete vehicle search.
![](../Files/Tray%202.3%2056.jpg)

I worked as an auditor on the development of two apartment buildings, River Lot 5 or as we said, Liver Rot 5. The pay was great. I became a taxpayer that year while still a student.

![](../Files/Tray%202.2%2032.jpg)

It was my first experience of the midnight sun. On June 21, it was twilight at midnight.

Explored Alberta with my 3 student colleagues

Visited the Woodwards in Edmonton

Still some evidence from the WWII [Canol Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canol_Project) in Waterways. Equipment and supplies were transferred from Northern Alberta Railway trains to barges for the trip down river to Norman Wells.
![](../Files/IMG_20170102_0027.jpg)

![](../Files/Tray%202.1%2043.jpg)

I had to climb something and the tower construction crane was right there. #ADHD
