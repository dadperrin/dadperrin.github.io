# Refuelling the S64 Skycrane

![Noon at Pullen Is International Airport](Noon%20at%20Pullen%20Is%20Int_l%20Airport.jpg)

The other side of ADHD. The Skycrane burned fuel faster than it could be fueled with gravity system. They didn't want to shut it down for refuelling at -40C.
Pressure refuelling at [Pullen Island International Airport](Pullen%20Island%20International%20Airport.md) was the solution
[Dave Boone](Dave%20Boone.md) asked me to do it because he knew I could.
I just called up the Esso Aviation folks at the airport and asked them what I needed for the refuelling skid. It was put together at a welding shop in Devon and shipped up on [IJR](IJR.md)
