# Refuelling the S64 Skycrane

![Noon at Pullen Is International Airport](Noon%20at%20Pullen%20Is%20Int_l%20Airport.jpg)

The Skycrane burned fuel faster than it could be fueled with existing gravity system. They didn't want to shut it down for refuelling at -40C.
Pressure refuelling at [Pullen Island International Airport](Pullen%20Island%20International%20Airport.md) was the solution.
My boss [Dave](Dave%20Boone.md) asked me to figure it out because he knew I could.

This is the other side of ADHD. I just called up the Esso Aviation folks at the airport and asked them what I needed for the refuelling skid. I ordered all the parts. It was put together at a welding shop in Devon and shipped up on [IJR](IJR.md).

The photo was taken at noon on Dec 21.
