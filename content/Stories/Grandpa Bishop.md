# Grandpa Bishop

I spent a lot of time with Grandpa Bishop before I started school because when Ian had an asthma attack, that's where I went.

![\|465x303](with%20Grandpa%20Bishop.jpg)

I remember him as  a quiet gentle man. We often walked to the train station in Mount Royal and took the train into downtown Montreal for a couple of hours and then went back.

His dad, Israel Bishop, was Fire Captain of station 13 in Montreal. They lived upstairs and he told me how he'd slide down the fire pole to get to school.
