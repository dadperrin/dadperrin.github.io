# Jumping from ship to ship

To do my job offshore, the only way I could get around most of the time to check on the other ships or get to one of the islands was to jump from the ship I was on to one of the crew boats and jump off the crew boat when I got to where I was going. The other options were helicopter and crane basket and they get their own stories. I got good at watching the waves for timing otherwise I would not be typing this. Unfortunately, one of the deck hands on a workboat got trapped between two ships and was killed instantly. Think "bug on a windshield".
